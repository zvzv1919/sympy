# sympy/benchmarks — Catalog

> Part of [SymPy](../catalog.md). Performance benchmarks for SymPy internals.

## Python Files

| File | Summary |
|------|---------|
| `bench_discrete_log.py` | Benchmarks discrete logarithm algorithms (trial multiplication, baby-step giant-step, Pollard rho, Pohlig-Hellman) over cyclic groups of varying size. |
| `bench_meijerint.py` | Benchmarks Meijer G-function based integration, including Mellin, Laplace, and Fourier transforms, and symbolic expectation integrals over statistical distributions. |
| `bench_symbench.py` | Runs a suite of symbolic computation benchmarks (polynomial expansion, Hermite polynomials, simplification, numerical substitution, Riemann sums) adapted from standard CAS comparison tests. |
